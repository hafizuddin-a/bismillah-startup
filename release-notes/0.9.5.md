Quicker greeting after sign-in and after waking.

**Install** (open PowerShell and paste this):

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hafizuddin-a/bismillah-startup/main/get.ps1 | iex
```

No admin rights needed. If you already have Bismillah, it will offer this update itself (**Check for Updates…** in the tray menu).

**What changed**
- **After waking:** the bismillah starts writing sooner after you sign in with your password, PIN or fingerprint. The app was treating the Windows sign-in program as "still locked" for a while after you were already in; it now relies on the lock screen itself. It also looks more often and waits less, but still never draws over the lock screen.
- **At sign-in:** the greeting starts as soon as the app is ready, instead of after a fixed pause.
- The log (`%APPDATA%\Bismillah\bismillah.log`) now also says how long after your desktop appeared Windows started the app, which shows how much of any delay is Windows holding startup apps back.

See the [README](https://github.com/hafizuddin-a/bismillah-startup#readme).
