Bismillah for Windows 1.0.

**Install** (open PowerShell and paste this):

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hafizuddin-a/bismillah-startup/main/get.ps1 | iex
```

No admin rights needed. If you already have Bismillah, it will offer this update itself (**Check for Updates…** in the tray menu).

**What it does**
- Writes out the bismillah, like handwriting, when you open the app and every time your PC wakes, once you have signed in (password, PIN or fingerprint).
- Optional dhikr reminders from the top of the screen at random times through the day, with your own hours and count, in the same lettering as the Mac app.
- Tray icon with Launch at Login, Show on All Screens and update options. The menu stays open while you change settings.
- Updates itself from GitHub releases, checked against a signature.

**Since the first preview**
- The dhikr text matches the Mac app.
- Waking from sleep and starting at sign-in wait for the lock screen to go, and are quicker.
- The menu stays open for settings.
- A log at `%APPDATA%\Bismillah\bismillah.log` for tracing problems.

**Known limits:** Windows holds sign-in apps back for several seconds, so the greeting appears a little after your desktop; the popup can't be drawn above the Start menu or a full-screen game; multiple monitors with different scaling are only tested on the build machine. Please [report anything odd](https://github.com/hafizuddin-a/bismillah-startup/issues).
