First preview of Bismillah for Windows.

**Install** (open PowerShell and paste this):

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hafizuddin-a/bismillah-startup/main/get.ps1 | iex
```

No admin rights needed. Or download the zip below and put `Bismillah.exe` in one of your own folders.

**What it does**
- Writes out the bismillah, like handwriting, when you open the app and every time your PC wakes (waiting for you to sign in if the lid opens onto the lock screen).
- Optional dhikr reminders from the top of the screen at random times through the day, with your own hours and count.
- Tray icon with Launch at Login, Show on All Screens and update options.
- Updates itself from GitHub releases, checked against a signature.

**This is a preview.** The animation, popups, menu and updater are tested on a real Windows machine in the build pipeline. Waking from sleep, the lock screen and multiple monitors still need a real-PC test, so please [report anything odd](https://github.com/hafizuddin-a/bismillah-startup/issues).

See the [README](https://github.com/hafizuddin-a/bismillah-startup#readme).
